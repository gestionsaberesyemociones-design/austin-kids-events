The city story

A little world with no wrong turn.

The setup combines foot-powered ride-on cars, kid-sized roadways, traffic signs and interactive play elements in one organized space designed for active, open-ended play. Kids can drive, stop, turn, follow the road—or completely ignore it and invent their own route.

Families can choose between different Mini City configurations, from colorful classic ride-on vehicles to a more polished collection of luxury-style cars.

Each setup is delivered and fully arranged before your event, then picked up afterward. Rentals are available for up to 12 hours, and selected activity or entertainment add-ons can be included depending on the celebration.

Available for birthdays, family gatherings and children's events throughout the Greater Austin area.
