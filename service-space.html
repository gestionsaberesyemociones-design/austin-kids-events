The mission briefing

A little universe built for big imaginations.

The setup includes four kid-powered space rovers, astronaut helmets, a Space Command Center, moon-inspired scenery and balance-play elements, all arranged together to create one interactive environment rather than a collection of separate toys.

Designed especially for ages 3-5, children can drive their rovers, report back to command, cross the "lunar surface" and create their own missions as the party unfolds.

The complete setup is delivered and arranged before your event, then picked up afterward. Rentals are available for up to 12 hours, with selected activity and entertainment add-ons available depending on your celebration.

Available for birthdays and children's events throughout the Greater Austin area.
