OUR STORY & MISSION

At Austin Kids Events, we believe every childhood celebration deserves real adventure and endless smiles. We bring immersive, screen-free themed vehicle experiences directly to your backyard, park, or party venue across Greater Austin.

From little cowpokes tending the farm to brave astronauts charting distant galaxies and mini city drivers navigating bustling streets, our custom-themed setups are designed to spark pure imagination, active movement, and unforgettable family memories.
